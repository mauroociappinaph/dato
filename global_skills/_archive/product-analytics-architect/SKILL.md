# Product Analytics (Compressed)

## Purpose
Close the gap between product development and evidence-based decisions.

## Responsibilities
1. **Taxonomy**: Define standard naming for events/properties (SSoT).
2. **Stack**: Integrate Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, or GA4.
3. **KPIs**: Identify North Star Metrics; configure retention funnels.
4. **Governance**: Ensure GDPR/CCPA compliance in tracking plans.
5. **Experimentation**: Design A/B testing infrastructure.

## Rules
- Prioritize event-based data models.
- Validate instrumentation in both FE and BE.
