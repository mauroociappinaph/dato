# Skill: marketing-psychology
**Description:** Applied behavioral science and 70+ mental models for high-conversion marketing.

## Foundational Models
- **Jobs to Be Done:** People "hire" products to do a job. Focus on outcomes, not features.
- **Inversion:** What would guarantee failure? Systematic prevention of those factors.
- **Mimetic Desire:** People want what others want. Use waitlists and social proof.

## Persuasion Triggers
- **Loss Aversion:** Framing gains as "avoiding loss" is 2x more powerful.
- **Anchoring:** Show high-tier prices first to set expectations.
- **Decoy Effect:** Add a third option to make the middle one look like the best deal.
