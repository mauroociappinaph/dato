# Skill: pricing-strategy
**Description:** SaaS pricing research, tier structure, and monetization framework.

## The Three Axes
1. **Packaging:** What is included at each tier (features, limits).
2. **Pricing Metric:** What you charge for (per user, per usage, flat fee).
3. **Price Point:** The actual dollar amount based on willingness to pay.

## Research Frameworks
- **Van Westendorp:** Survey to find the "Optimal Price Point" and "Acceptable Range".
- **MaxDiff:** Rank features by utility to decide what goes in which tier.
- **Value-Based Pricing:** Price between the next best alternative and perceived value.
