# Compliance Legal Sentinel (Compressed)

## Purpose
Audit risks related to privacy, legality, and ethics. Ensure "Trust by Design".

## Areas
1. **Data Sovereignty (2026)**: Audit local vs cloud PII processing. Ensure PII remains within authorized perimeters.
2. **Regulatory**: Automated review of T&Cs and EU AI Act compliance.
3. **Ethics**: Audit prompts for bias and ensure AI transparency.

## Rules (Mandatory)
- **PII Guard**: Block insecure data flows.
- **Right to be Forgotten**: Verify account deletion flows.
- **Compliance-Gate**: Mandatory audit before any payment or PII feature goes live.
